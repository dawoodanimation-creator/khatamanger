# Shopkeeper personas

These are design assumptions for this audit, not interviewed or statistically representative users.

## 1. Nadeem — busy kiryana shop owner

42, Rawalpindi. Medium phone skill; reads Roman Urdu more comfortably than English. Uses a cheap Android phone one-handed while serving a queue, often in bright sunlight with unreliable mobile data. His son sometimes uses the same phone. He needs to find a familiar customer and record udhaar/payment in about ten seconds. He fears choosing the wrong Ali, adding an extra zero, or losing the ledger. Clear customer identity, readable amounts, immediate save feedback and a simple backup matter most.

## 2. Shazia — neighbourhood general-store operator

35, Multan. Low phone skill; reads Urdu and uses WhatsApp, but does not understand “JSON” or browser storage. Small screen, Urdu keyboard, limited data and a shared family phone. She records small credits between other tasks and may pause mid-entry. She needs obvious “customer owes me / I owe customer” labels, large reachable controls, a way to recover from mistakes and privacy from casual viewers. She cannot be expected to clear browser storage or troubleshoot an unreadable backup.
